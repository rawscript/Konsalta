Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Konsalta, the work we do, and how to start a conversation with our team.

What is Konsalta?

Konsalta is a women-led, Africa-focused research, consulting, and advisory firm. We work with organisations to translate evidence into action, strengthen institutional capability, and support lasting impact.

Who does Konsalta work with?

We work with governments and public institutions, development partners and international organisations, private sector organisations, academic and research institutions, and civil society.

What services does Konsalta provide?

Our work spans research and evaluation, strategy and advisory, market intelligence and insights, and policy and systems. Visit Our Services for more detail.

Where is Konsalta based, and where does it work?

Konsalta is based in Harare, Zimbabwe, and is focused on work across Africa and beyond, combining local and regional insight with a global perspective.

How does Konsalta tailor its work to each organisation?

We shape our approach around each organisation’s mandate and needs. Our agile, network-driven model brings together the relevant core team members and specialist expertise for the work.

How can I discuss a project with Konsalta?

Use the Contact Us page to share your enquiry and the support you are looking for. Our team can then discuss the appropriate next steps.

How can a consultant express interest in joining the network?

Visit the Join Our Network page for information about the consultant network. The page currently notes that online submission is unavailable; you can contact Konsalta to ask about next steps.
