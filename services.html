Thematic focus areas

Where we apply our capabilities across Africa

We bring these capabilities to life across the key policy, development, institutional, and commercial challenges and opportunities shaping Africa’s future.

  1. Macroeconomics and Economic Policy

    Supporting evidence-based economic policymaking, fiscal management, labour market analysis, economic transformation, and inclusive growth.

  2. Governance and Public Policy

    Strengthening institutions, improving public service delivery, and promoting effective, accountable, and responsive governance.

  3. Data Governance, Statistics and Digital Transformation

    Strengthening data governance frameworks, statistical systems, and digital ecosystems to support decision-making, accountability, and innovation.

  4. Poverty, Inequality, Livelihoods and Social Protection

    Advancing policies and programmes that reduce poverty, expand economic opportunities, strengthen livelihoods, and improve resilience among vulnerable populations.

  5. Human Development and Demography

    Improving outcomes in health, education, skills development, human capital, population dynamics, urbanisation, and demographic change.

  6. Gender, Diversity & Inclusion

    Embedding equality metrics, workplace diversity frameworks, and social safeguards into large-scale organizational models.

  7. Forced Displacement, Migration and Inclusion

    Supporting evidence-based responses to displacement, migration, refugee inclusion, and social cohesion.

  8. Private Sector Development and Market Systems

    Supporting enterprise development, market systems strengthening, value chain development, financial inclusion, and investment readiness.

  9. Corporate Performance and Commercial Growth

    Supporting businesses with agile scaling strategies, executive alignment, resilient leadership pipelines, and investment-ready narrative positioning.

  10. Climate Change and Environmental Resilience

    Supporting climate adaptation, resilience, sustainability, and climate-informed policy, planning, and investment decisions.
