Guiding Methodology
“No two assignments are the same. Each client faces a unique combination of challenges, stakeholders, and opportunities. We bring seven principles together, one assignment at a time.”

Seven Principles, One Assignment at a Time

Explore the seven operational principles that govern how we scope, assemble, execute, and deliver lasting value for every client partnership.

01.

Start with the problem, not predetermined solutions.

We initiate every engagement by understanding the client’s core challenge, their operating environment, identifying key stakeholders, critical constraints and desired outcomes.

Deep diagnostic inquiry & stakeholder mapping
02.

Build the right team.

We assemble tailored, multidisciplinary teams for each assignment, combining the expertise needed to deliver results with clear leadership and accountability.

Bespoke multidisciplinary squads under senior leadership
03.

Generate credible evidence.

We apply rigorous, fit-for-purpose methods grounded in professional standards, tailored to align with the unique objectives and context of each client.

Scientific methodologies & transparent verification
04.

Bring context to the evidence.

We combine analytical rigor with knowledge of African policy, institutional, market, social and cultural contexts to interpret findings and how they can realistically be applied.

Cultural alignment & institutional empathy
05.

Translate evidence into action.

We turn knowledge into recommendations, policies, strategies, implementation plans, communication approaches and other practical actions.

Action-oriented frameworks & executive blueprints
06.

Strengthen capacity as we deliver.

Where appropriate, we work alongside client teams and emerging professionals, transferring knowledge and strengthening skills throughout the assignment. This enables the value of an engagement to extend beyond the delivery of a final product.

Capability transfer & internal skills enhancement
07.

Engage continuously throughout the process.

We maintain continuous engagement with clients, creating opportunities to test assumptions, review emerging findings, and stay aligned with changing circumstances.

Transparent governance & collaborative iteration
