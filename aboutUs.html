Our Approach

Seven principles, one assignment at a time

No two assignments are the same. Each client faces a unique combination of challenges, stakeholders, contexts and objectives. We bring together a strong core team and a wider multidisciplinary network to assemble the expertise each assignment needs.

01 / 07

Start with the problem.

We begin by understanding the client's core challenge and operating environment, identifying key stakeholders, critical constraints and desired outcomes.

02 / 07

Build the right team.

We assemble a multidisciplinary team for each assignment, bringing together the expertise needed to deliver results, with clear leadership and accountability.

03 / 07

Generate credible evidence.

We use rigorous, fit-for-purpose methods grounded in professional standards and tailored to each client's objectives and context.

04 / 07

Bring context to the evidence.

We combine analytical rigor with knowledge of African policy, institutional, market, social and cultural contexts to interpret findings and assess how they can be applied in practice.

05 / 07

Translate evidence into action.

We turn knowledge into practical recommendations, policies, strategies, implementation plans and communication approaches.

06 / 07

Strengthen capacity as we deliver.

Where appropriate, we work alongside client teams and emerging professionals, sharing knowledge and strengthening skills throughout the assignment. This helps the value of our work extend beyond the final deliverable.

07 / 07

Engage throughout the process.

We stay closely engaged with clients, creating opportunities to test assumptions, review emerging findings and adapt as circumstances change.
